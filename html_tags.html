2026 Israeli–United States strikes on Iran

On 28 February 2026, Israel and the United States launched a joint attack on various sites in Iran. The operation, codenamed Roaring Lion[a] by Israel[34][35] and Operation Epic Fury by the United States Department of Defense,[36][37] targeted key officials, military commanders, and facilities.
The attack included the assassination of the second Supreme Leader of Iran, Ali Khamenei.[38]
